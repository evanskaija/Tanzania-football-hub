04 / NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Find a quick answer or send the office a message if you need more help.

View All FAQs →
How can I contact the Tanzania Premier League Board?

Email the board at Info@ligikuu.co.tz or use the message form above.

Where is the TPLB office located?

The office is on the 14th Floor of NSSF Mafao House, Plot No. 40, Block Y, Ilala, Dar es Salaam.

How can clubs contact the league?

Clubs can use the official league email and select Clubs or Competition Information in the enquiry type.

Where can I report an issue with fixtures or results?

Select Fixtures & Results in the message form and include the match details so the office can route the enquiry.

How can media organisations contact TPLB?

Select Media & Press or email the board with your publication and request details.
